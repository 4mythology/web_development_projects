Robotics

What is Robotics?

The word robot comes from the Czech word robota (which means 'forced labour') and the term was first used in the 1920s play Rossum's Universal Robots. The concept of the first robo has fired the imagination of science fiction writers for countless years.

The three laws of robotics are a set of ethical principles for robots and artificial intelligenct. They were devised by the Science fiction writer Isaac Asimov and first appeared in his 1942 short story Runaround

The laws are:

  • A robot may not injure a human through action or inaction
  • A robot must obey orders given by humans, unless it comes into conflics with law 1
  • A robot must protect itself, unless this conflicts with law 1

Uses of Robots

In factories:

  • Welding parts
  • Spray-painting cars
  • fitting windscreens
  • bottling and labelling
  • Automatic location of items

In the home:

  • Automated sweeper
  • Autonomous mower
  • Ironing robots
  • Automatic window cleaner
  • Entertainment

Drones:

  • UAVs
  • reconnaissance
  • parcel deliveries

Characteristics of a robot

To be correctly called a robot, a machine has to have the following characteristics:

  • Ability to sense their surroundings
  • Having a degree of movement
  • Programmable
