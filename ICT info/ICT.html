Information and Communication Technology

What is ICT?

ICT is the use of computing and telecommunication technologies to facilitate the creation, collection, processing, transmission, and storage of information. It encompasses a wide range of tools and resources, including the internet, wireless networks, cell phones, and other communication mediums.

How is ICT used?

ICT has many uses in our daily lives. These include:

  • Communication: Bridging Distances
  • Education: Knowledge Anytime, Anywhere
  • Healthcaare: Remote Care for All
  • Entertainment: Endless Choices at Your Fingertips
  • Work and Productivity: Redefining the Workplace
  • Financial Management: Convenient and Secure Transactions
  • Home Automation: Smart Living
  • Transportation: Navigating Efficiently
  • Accessibility and Inclusivity: Empowering All
  • Energy Efficiency and Smart Grids

Where is ICT used?

Nowadays, ICT is used everywhere, in various fields, including E-commerce, E-governance, Banking, Agriculture, Education, Medicine, Defense, and Transport.
